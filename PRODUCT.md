# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Hotel and restaurant F&B teams in Türkiye and similar markets: executive chefs, cost controllers, F&B managers, and marketing leads who own recipes, menu pricing, allergens, and compliance signals. They work under time pressure, often between service periods, with finance and audit asking for numbers that match the pass.

## Product Purpose

Carta is an F&B operations studio: one editorial workspace for recipes, moving-average cost, menu pricing, menu engineering, variance audit, and bilingual export. Success means trusted per-dish economics, codex-aligned nutrition data, and menus that can be priced and defended without spreadsheet chaos.

## Brand Personality

Considered, craft-led, expert but calm. Editorial like a serious kitchen notebook, not a flashy startup. Confident in numbers and regulation; warm through material quality (typography, photography, restraint), not through cheerleading copy.

## Anti-references

- Generic purple-gradient SaaS dashboards and "AI slop" cream landing pages
- All-caps body copy, marketing buzzwords (streamline, empower, seamless, world-class)
- Card stacks inside card stacks, nested UI chrome for its own sake
- Treating kitchen economics as back-office decoration instead of part of the craft
- Loud hero type above 6rem or cramped display letter-spacing

## Design Principles

1. **Construct, don't decorate.** Every screen earns its place; metrics answer questions chefs and controllers actually ask.
2. **Measured, considered, complete.** One mental model from recipe through export; no vanity charts.
3. **Economics are craft.** Cost, margin, and compliance belong in the same story as the dish, not in a separate "admin" aesthetic.
4. **Editorial hierarchy.** Playfair for display voice, Barlow for work surfaces; sentence case on buttons and labels.
5. **Compliance is visible, not hidden.** TGK Ek-10/Ek-13, KVKK, VUK, HACCP signals stay legible where relevant.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for text and controls. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (motion tokens and transitions already gated in CSS). Body text must stay readable on tinted surfaces (ink-graphite on bone/sand, not washed-out gray). TR/EN/ES copy; hospitality wording uses "guest" / "misafir", not "customer". Outlook-safe email patterns where applicable for property comms.
